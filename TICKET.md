# MF-2104 Reporting: close the books each month

**Type:** Story · **Team:** Finance tools · **Reporter:** Product (Finance)

As a finance manager, I want a monthly report of my sales, fees and refunds that I can export to my accounting software, so that I can close the books accurately each month.

**Acceptance criteria**

- The report covers one calendar month.
- It shows gross sales, fees, refunds, and net.
- Refunds count in the month they were issued.

**Notes:** Until now, finance has counted refunds in the month of the original sale.
