# MF-1514 Pay the merchant

**Type:** Story · **Team:** Payments · **Reporter:** Product (Payouts)

Merchants are paid from their balance. The available balance has cleared. Captures that have not cleared sit in pending.

New merchants are on a reserve. Risk releases it. Finance has been paying them after a week live, and holding the rest until risk clears the account. Before that release, some payouts go out at half the balance.
