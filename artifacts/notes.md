- When is the payment one of this merchant's? Only when request.merchantId equals payment.merchantId and request.paymentId equals payment.id; otherwise 404 payment not found, before any status or amount rule.
- Which payments can be refunded? succeeded and pending. Pending is included because support already refunds pending payments from the dashboard, and this API lets the merchant do that from their own system. failed (and any other status) is 409, with error "payment failed" for a failed payment.
- What counts as more than the payment? The requested amount plus cents already refunded. The cap is payment.amountCents minus payment.refundedCents. A request over that remaining balance is refused in full with 400 refund exceeds payment, and is not reduced to the amount left.
- What is refunded when the merchant does not say how much? The remaining balance, so the whole payment ends up refunded. Null is treated as omitted. An explicit amount must be a positive whole number of cents; zero, negative, and fractional amounts are 400 invalid amount.
- What if nothing is left, or the payment figures are not whole cents? 409. "already refunded" when some cents were already refunded, "nothing to refund" when the payment itself has nothing refundable, and "payment is not refundable" when the stored amounts are negative or not whole cents.
- Which response says the refund went through? 201, refundedCents is only the amount this request refunded, and refundId is `re_` plus the request id. No refund id is returned when nothing is refunded, and the payment record is left unchanged.
